Trades one, two and three were on the list. They followed the plan and they finished +$310. Trade four was opened at 12:20 — 40 minutes after the session was supposed to be over — and it cost -$1,920. The day closed at -$1,610.

That part every trader remembers. The next part is the one nobody has: the month. Six red days. Five of them had a fourth trade. The green days almost never did.

Across 214 trades the first three of a session win 64% of the time. From the fourth on, it drops to 31%. Same trader, same setups, same account — a different hour and a decision that was already made.

The strategy was never the variable. The trade count was. And you cannot see that in a broker statement, only in a record that knows which trade of the day it was.
