Trades one, two and three were on the list. They followed the plan and they finished +$310.

Trade four was not on the list. It was opened 40 minutes after the session was supposed to be over, in a setup you would not have taken at 9am, in a size you picked because the day was almost green. It cost -$1,920.

The strategy was never in the room for that one. The clock was.

You remember the trade. You have never counted it. Ask yourself how many fourth trades are in the last hundred, what they cost, and what time of day they happen — and notice that you can't answer any of it from memory.

That is the whole job of a journal. Not the win rate. The fourth trade.

Figures are an illustrative example, not real results.
